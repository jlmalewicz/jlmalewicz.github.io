astronomical instruments series

ALMA

Radio array, part of the Event Horizon Telescope

IceCube

Neutrino detector embedded in the polar ice caps

JWST

Optical and infrared space telescope

LIGO

Gravitational wave detector, along with Virgo and KAGRA

LISA

Future space-based gravitational wave detector

NewAthena

Future X-ray space telescope

Rubin

Large optical survey instrument based in Chile. Also the first major telescope named after a woman

Tech Tower

Clock tower on the Georgia Tech campus

Trinity

Array of neutrino detectors based in Utah
